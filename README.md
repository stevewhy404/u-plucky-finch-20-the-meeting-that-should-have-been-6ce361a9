One line, at most 200 characters, that makes a meeting-that-should-have-been-an-email funny.

## Entry

> We held a 47-minute meeting to decide the email was too long, then scheduled another meeting to discuss shortening it.

`main.py` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` (verified 1–200 UTF-8 characters, one line, not only whitespace).
